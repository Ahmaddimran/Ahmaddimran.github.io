[Why Classifier-Free Guidance Is Fragile Under Quantization: A Mechanistic Study](https://openreview.net/forum?id=kOxMYOcSn4)

**Ahmad Imran** · NeurIPS 2026 AXIOM Workshop

This paper explains why even small quantization errors can visibly degrade images generated with classifier-free guidance. It separates the errors into two parts: errors shared by the conditional and unconditional predictions, and errors that differ between them. Classifier-free guidance amplifies the differing errors, which can produce visible artifacts.
